# Teams Emoji Pack

A free starter pack of custom emojis for Microsoft Teams, Slack and Discord, pulled from the [Teamsmoji](https://www.teamsmoji.com) library of 100,000+ emojis.

## What's here

Microsoft Teams, Slack and Discord each have different emoji size limits, which is why most "emoji resizer" tools (built for Twitch) get it wrong. [Teamsmoji](https://www.teamsmoji.com) fixes that:

- 100,000+ free custom emojis, memes and reaction GIFs, searchable and organized into packs
- Every emoji downloads at the right size for Teams, Slack or Discord
- A free [Emoji Maker](https://www.teamsmoji.com/emoji-maker) that turns any image, GIF or video into a perfectly sized emoji, entirely in your browser
- Short guides on adding custom emojis to each platform

## Quick start

1. Browse the library at [teamsmoji.com](https://www.teamsmoji.com)
2. Pick an emoji (party parrot, blob cats, sports, reactions...)
3. Download for your platform and upload it in your workspace settings

No login, no watermark, nothing leaves your device in the maker.
